I build accelerator control and beam simulation software at the Budker Institute of Nuclear Physics and at the SKIF synchrotron radiation facility. I wrote SCAUT and ACCUMULATOR — frameworks for automating experiments on the SKIF injector, with digital twins on Elegant, EPICS IOC and Docker — and SAVA, a conversational LLM assistant for operators. I compute beam envelopes and trajectories with relativistic difference schemes in Python and C++, and optimise them with genetic algorithms and Bayesian optimisation.

bio
Vyacheslav Fedorov is a junior researcher at the Budker Institute of Nuclear Physics SB RAS. He works on beam dynamics and control systems for the SKIF injector, and separately on autonomous agents for working with data. He teaches at Novosibirsk State University and at a gymnasium. He lives in Novosibirsk and is raising two sons.
projects
SAVA 2026 — an assistant for accelerator operators: Telegram bot, web and command line. It searches the documentation through RAG, polls EPICS channels and keeps the shift log. GitHub
ACCUMULATOR 2026 — an accelerator emulator. Configurations and control loops are tried on it before machine time at SKIF. GitHub · Registration no. 2026668488
SCAUT 2025 — automation of accelerator experiments: scans, tuning loops, data collection and analysis. During commissioning of the SKIF injector it drove automatic orbit correction by three methods — response matrices, Bayesian optimisation and least squares — and full current transmission was achieved in the linac and the booster. Described in a JINST paper. GitHub · Registration no. 2025619977
Autonomous AI researcher 2025 — an "AI scientist" for business problems: it forms hypotheses itself and tests them with queries against corporate data. It grew out of the SberLab–NSU summer school together with SberData: the method was first tried on accelerator orbit correction, and on Text-to-SQL the solution holds third place on the BIRD benchmark at 81.33 % accuracy, alongside teams from corporate research labs. Paper in Doklady Mathematics
KENV 2024 — solves the Kapchinsky–Vladimirsky envelope equation with space charge. GitHub · PyPI · Wiki · Registration no. 2024611244
REDPIC 2023 — a particle-in-cell code in Python for relativistic particle dynamics. For cases where the envelope is not enough and kinetics are needed. GitHub · PyPI
Compass FSS Link 2023 — backend for a non-profit financial services organisation: Django, DRF, PostgreSQL, Celery, RabbitMQ, Docker. Website
